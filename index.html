Lyrics Studio
A simple browser editor for timed lyrics and subtitles
  1. Choose a video or song
  2. Paste the lyrics, one line per subtitle
  3. Time them with AI, or split evenly
  4. Fix lines on the timeline
  5. Export the video

Have the vocals as a separate file?
Some song tools, for example Suno, can give you the vocals without the music. AI timing then listens to that file instead of the full song, which is much more accurate. The video still uses the full song.
Time with AI listens to the song and finds when each line of your lyrics is sung. It works with any language. The first time, it downloads a 300 MB model, which can take a few minutes; after that the browser keeps it.
Tap along plays the song while you hold a button during each line. It takes one listen and is the most exact way when the AI gets lines wrong.
Split evenly gives every line the same length, so you can set the times yourself.
